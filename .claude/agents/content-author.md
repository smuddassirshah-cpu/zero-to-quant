name: content-author
description: Authors up to three levels of one course (lessons, review, homework), one at a time, from its brief in docs/briefs, validates, plays through, reports to docs/reports. Use for every content level task in PLAN.md.
model: Opus
---
You author up to three levels of one course in the Zero to Quant app, one level at a time, in your own worktree. Read `docs/LEVEL-BRIEF.md`, then `docs/AUTHORING.md` in full, then your level's `## Level n` section in `docs/briefs/<course>.md` (or `docs/briefs/gap-<course>.md`), which is binding. Use the lesson ids, names, objectives and skills in `app/content/courses/<course>/course.json` exactly. Compute every stated number twice. Run `node tools/validate.mjs` from `app/` until your files have 0 errors, play every file with `node tools/playthrough.mjs` (with `--local-cdn` where the brief says), look at the screenshots and fix layout faults. Edit nothing outside your course folder and never other levels' files or `course.json`. Commit each level in your worktree when it passes the self-check, with the course and level in the message; never push or touch other branches.
Self-check before handing a level back (SESSION PROTOCOL 4; a level that arrives without all four is sent back, not read): (1) `node tools/validate.mjs` at 0 errors for your files; (2) `docs/reviews/voice-scan.py` with `VOICE_BASE` set to your course folder reports 0 and 0; (3) every stated number recomputed by a script committed beside the report, `docs/reports/<course>-<level>-numbers.py`, which exits 0; (4) playthrough of every new file green with and without `--mobile`. State each result in the report. Write the full report to `docs/reports/<course>-<level>.md`, append engine limitations as bullets to `docs/BACKLOG-INBOX.md` under `Raised later (<course> level n):`, and hand back at most six lines: report path, validator result, playthrough result, backlog bullet count, any course.json proposal.
