# Interview gaps: what quant internship processes test, against what the app holds

Planner's analysis for PLAN.md task "Interview gap analysis and plan". The plan that closes these gaps is `docs/briefs/interview-plan.md`.

**Sources and their limits.** Every process detail below is candidate-reported (Glassdoor, 1point3acres, QuantNet, forum posts) or comes from prep sites (QuantVault, TraderMath, Quantt, TechInterview, TradingInterview), unless marked "official". Prep sites sell preparation, and their figures disagree. Formats change every cycle and differ by office. The web fetch tool could not reach these hosts from this session (quantvault.org and janestreet.com did not resolve), so the details come from search-engine summaries of the pages listed. Questions are paraphrased, never quoted. Read every row as "reported", not "confirmed".

**Snapshot date:** 29 October 2026. The summer 2027 cycle opened in July and August 2026 at most of these firms, with rolling deadlines from October onwards ([QuantVault timeline](https://quantvault.org/quant-internship-timeline-2027.html), [Extern guide](https://www.extern.com/post/quant-internships-summer-2027-guide), [Quantt guide](https://www.quantt.co.uk/resources/quant-internships-guide), [TechInterview timeline](https://www.techinterview.org/post/3233474727/wall-street-internship-recruiting-timeline/)).

**Patterns across firms.**

- Trading firms (Jane Street, Optiver, SIG, IMC, Citadel Securities) test probability, expected value, mental arithmetic and live market-making games. Their OAs are speed batteries. Their interviews are conversations in which reasoning aloud is marked.
- Research roles (Two Sigma, Citadel, JP Morgan QR) test statistics, regression, machine learning and an open-ended data case, plus coding. Two Sigma's OA is a data and regression coding test.
- Banks (JP Morgan, Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley) gate with HackerRank coding and a recorded video interview. Strats add a maths multiple-choice section, and older desk-quant reports add stochastic calculus and derivatives.
- Developer roles (HRT, Two Sigma, Citadel and Jane Street SWE) test LeetCode-medium coding at speed, layered design problems and, at HRT and Citadel Securities, C++ and systems depth.
- Every firm asks behavioural and motivation questions, and banks and Optiver gate on them early (HireVue, recruiter call).
