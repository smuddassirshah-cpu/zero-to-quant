# Level briefs: representations (Embeddings and Generative Models, topic 2.7)

Course header. `representations` is the fourth course of topic 2.7, Deep learning, strand `ai` ("Not needed for the quant path. Machine learning research roles, and reading papers on embeddings and generative models."). It has 5 levels and 20 lessons, skills `rp-embed`, `rp-autoenc`, `rp-vae`, `rp-diffuse` and `rp-graph`, one per level. The binding curriculum is `docs/briefs/ai-strand-curriculum.md` section 2.7; the findings behind it are in `docs/reviews/review-ai-strand.md` (F6 added `info`, which level 3 needs; F7 added the `eigen` L4 edge for the multivariate normal; F12 split the old generative level into the VAE and diffusion and added graph networks).
