**Pure-course rule (binding for every level).** A free-form proof is not gradable, so proof is taught by guided steps. Each lesson paragraph below says which widget grades each step of each proof. The tools are: `order` (arrange the lines of a proof; every line carries its own justification, as `docs/AUTHORING.md` section 6 asks; `same` for interchangeable steps); `choice` or `multi` (pick the justification for a step, choose which axiom fails, decide whether a claim follows); spot the flawed line, as a `table` canvas with `tap: "rows"` (one proof line per row) and a `tap-regions` widget whose answer is the bad row, or as a `choice` naming the line when the table does not render the line well; fill-in widgets for the computed parts (`numeric`, `expression`, and `table` for a matrix in a basis, a coordinate vector or a list of dimensions); `manipulate` for a process such as the exchange argument; `drag-point` on a `vectors` canvas where a picture fits. Whole proofs appear only as `longform` homework with a 10-point rubric and a full model answer. Each lesson has a `derive` screen that orders the lesson's main proof.

**Notation for the whole course (fix it in level 1 and keep it).** Field $\mathbb{F}$ ($\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$). Spaces $V, W, U$. Vectors bold lower case, $\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v}$, as in `vectors` and `elimination`; polynomials and functions stay plain, $p, f$. Lists in brackets, $(\mathbf{v}_1, \dots, \mathbf{v}_n)$. $\mathcal{P}_m(\mathbb{F})$ for polynomials of degree at most $m$, $\mathbb{F}^n$ for lists, $\mathbb{R}^{S}$ for functions on a set $S$. $\operatorname{span}$, $\dim$. Linear maps are italic capitals $T, S$ in $\mathcal{L}(V, W)$; matrices bold capitals $\mathbf{A}$. Write $\ker T$ and $\operatorname{im} T$ (say once that Axler writes null and range, and that they are the $N(\mathbf{A})$ and $C(\mathbf{A})$ of `elimination` l5 and l6). The matrix of $T$ from basis $\mathcal{B}$ to basis $\mathcal{C}$ is $[T]_{\mathcal{B}}^{\mathcal{C}}$, of an operator $[T]_{\mathcal{B}}$, and the coordinate vector $[\mathbf{v}]_{\mathcal{B}}$. Dual space $V'$, dual basis $\varphi_1, \dots, \varphi_n$, dual map $T'$ (Axler's), transpose $\mathbf{A}^T$. Inner product $\langle \mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v} \rangle$, norm $\|\mathbf{v}\|$ (the $|\mathbf{v}|$ of `vectors` l3), adjoint $T^*$, conjugate transpose $\mathbf{A}^*$. $U^\perp$ and $P_U$. $\mathrm{Var}$ and $\mathrm{Cov}$ with `\mathrm`.
