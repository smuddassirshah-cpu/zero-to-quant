**Notation for the whole course.** Vectors bold lower case ($\mathbf{x}$, $\mathbf{w}$, $\mathbf{m}$, $\mathbf{1}$), matrices bold capitals ($\mathbf{A}$, $\boldsymbol{\Sigma}$, $\mathbf{H}$ for a Hessian), as in `eigen`. A problem is written $\min f_0(\mathbf{x})$ subject to $f_i(\mathbf{x}) \le 0$ and $h_j(\mathbf{x}) = 0$ (Boyd's standard form), with Lagrangian $L = f_0 + \sum_i \lambda_i f_i + \sum_j \nu_j h_j$ and $\lambda_i \ge 0$. For portfolios keep the `multivar` letters: $\lambda$ on the budget $\mathbf{1}^T\mathbf{w} = 1$, $\mu$ on the return target $\mathbf{m}^T\mathbf{w} \ge r$, $\nu_i$ on $w_i \ge 0$ and $\eta_i$ on a cap $w_i \le c$, so stationarity reads $2\boldsymbol{\Sigma}\mathbf{w} = \lambda\mathbf{1} + \mu\mathbf{m} + \boldsymbol{\nu} - \boldsymbol{\eta}$ with $\mu, \nu_i, \eta_i \ge 0$ (say once in l10 how this matches Boyd's signs). A convex combination is $\theta\mathbf{a} + (1 - \theta)\mathbf{b}$ with $0 \le \theta \le 1$. Level 4 uses step size $s$ (as `multivar` l8), Hessian eigenvalues $\lambda_{\min}$ and $\lambda_{\max}$, and condition number $\kappa = \lambda_{\max}/\lambda_{\min}$.
