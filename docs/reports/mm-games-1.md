Every lesson: hook, explore, named term, ladder, trap, generalise, derive, code, ledger P&L drill, transfer, summary and quiz; one edge case each (l1 a known quantity, zero width; l2 the sum of 100 dice; l3 a known payout, zero width; l4 a zero factor on a holiday). Homework has the `order` item (clarify, estimate, range, quote, update, with a "work silently" distractor), the `longform` mock opening round on the chessboard (10-point rubric, full spoken model answer) paired with the `numeric` 204, and unseen interview games (digit sum of 1 to 100, distinct faces of three dice, a Fermi range, a two-dice ledger). Review items are unseen games too (sevens in 1 to 100, |X − Y|, the largest of three dice, a scaled sd, three-dice coverage 8 to 13, a rumour widening a quote, £1 coins in a mile, a mixed error factor, a chessboard ledger). The Jane Street Probability and Markets guide is named once (l1 transfer), Zhou's green book and Crack's *Heard on the Street* in l1 and l4; no text is quoted.

`tags`: the validator accepts a `tags` list on set items without complaint (it neither checks nor rejects it), so every review and homework item carries one family name (`digit-count`, `calibration`, `dice-ev`, `dice-sd`, `coverage`, `width-vol`, `fermi`, `fermi-error`, `ledger-pnl`, `dice-market-code`, `calibration-code`, `market-steps`, `chess-squares`, `mock-round`).

## Seeds and conventions

- £ throughout; contracts pay £1 a point unless stated. Ledger columns: Trade, Price (£), Position, P&L at fair (£), four columns so they fit at 390 px.
- Bars simulation seed 7 (two-dice explore). Python checks: `random.Random(2026)` (l1, l2 numbers), 11, 12, 13 (homework and review simulations), 200,000 trials each.
- Code screens are pure standard-library Python (`fractions`, `itertools`, `math`); exact answers are returned as `Fraction` and compared with `compare: "fraction"`.
- Homework quote rule: centre c = ⌊2μ + ½⌋/2 (nearest half, ties up), symmetric integer quotes (c − h, c + h), first with coverage ≥ 1/2, ends included, None once 2h exceeds the limit.
- Two-sided binomial p-value: the sum of all outcome probabilities no larger than that of the observed count, with `Fraction(str(level))` so ties compare exactly.
