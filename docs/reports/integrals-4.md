- l14: the irreducible quadratic $x^2 + 1$ (screen 6) and an already-simple fraction (quiz q-which-needs).

Traps: l14 splitting $\frac{x^2 + 1}{x^2 - 1}$ without dividing (the plane shows a gap of exactly 1; the repeated-factor $\frac{A}{x^2}$ alone is a quiz distractor); l15 keeping the $x$ limits and dropping the $\cos\theta$ of $dx$; l16 the fundamental theorem across $\int_{-1}^1 x^{-2}\,dx$ giving $-2$; l17 squaring the integral instead of the radius (and forgetting to square).

## Maths-engine equivalence

`eqcheck.mjs` calls `ZQ.grade.exprEqualUpToConstant` in the browser, with the local Compute Engine 0.30.2, on typed LaTeX forms. Accepted, for $\int \frac{dx}{x(x^2+1)}$ (target $\ln x - \frac12\ln(x^2+1)$): `\ln x-\frac12\ln(x^2+1)`, the same with `+C`, `\ln\left(\frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+1}}\right)`, `\ln x-0.5\ln(x^2+1)+c`, `\ln x-\ln\sqrt{x^2+1}`, `\ln|x|-\frac12\ln|x^2+1|`. Rejected: `\ln x-\ln(x^2+1)` and `\ln x-\arctan x`. Accepted, for $\int\sin^3x\,dx$ (target $-\cos x+\frac{\cos^3x}{3}$): the same form, `\frac13\cos^3x-\cos x+C`, `-\cos(x)+\frac13\cos(x)^3`, `+K`. Rejected: `\frac{\sin^4x}{4}`, `\cos x-\frac{\cos^3x}{3}`, `-\cos x+\cos^3x`. One form, `-\cos x(1-\frac{\cos^2x}{3})`, is read as $\cos$ of a product and rejected; that is a reasonable reading of the typed text. The plain-text `accept` entries do not match in the engine (see the backlog), so the LaTeX forms carry it.
