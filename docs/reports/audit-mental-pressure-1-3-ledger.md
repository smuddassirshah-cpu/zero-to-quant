Blind audit of `app/content/courses/mental-pressure/` levels 1 to 3 (l1 to l12, review-1 to review-3, homework-1 to homework-3). One row per definition, result, method step, convention, always/never/only claim, interpretation, causal claim, code-behaviour claim, and the reasoning of each explain, why, hint and feedback line (lines that restate the same reasoning share a row). Paths are JSON paths inside the file. Verdicts: correct, wrong, imprecise, misleading, unverified. `sim:` names a check in `docs/reports/mental-pressure-L<n>-concepts.py`.
