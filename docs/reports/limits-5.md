## Conventions

- Time on the stock path is in hours after 10:00; the path is smooth and seeded by its formula, not by a random generator.
- Rates are written $x'$ for $\tfrac{dx}{dt}$ (introduced on l18 screen 2).
- Money in £; basis points are 0.0001 (stated in feedback where a learner slips).
- Ladder screens in l18 use `table` canvases (quantity, value now, rate), so no ladder changes canvas kind. The ladder animation is a `plane` with a control bound to a free parameter `t` and a `derive` block.
