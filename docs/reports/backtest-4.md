## Conventions

- Data: `make_bars()` verbatim (seed 2301, 1,260 bars, 2 January 2019 to 31 October 2023). Twin: `make_bars(phi=0.0)`. `split_holdout(bars, 252, gap)`: research = first 1,008 bars (to 11 November 2022), held out = last 252 (14 November 2022 to 31 October 2023). Note: the held-out year is not calendar 2023; the lessons give the real dates.
- Each period is backtested on its own (no warm-up from research), 10,000 shares from £1m, 2.5 bp a trade, next-open fills.
- Lookback grid `LB = [1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 20, 40, 60]`. The 48-setting grid of l15: these 8 lookbacks × entry thresholds 0, 1%, 2% (flat when |lookback return| ≤ threshold) × follow or fade. The 8-lookback grid is its corner (threshold 0, follow), so the honest N for the course study is 48.
- l14's colleague engines `a` to `e` are vectorised in units (±1), lookback 1, 2.5 bp, returning `position`, `ret`, `cost`: a = signal and P&L both from the forward return; b = costs added; c = fade a centred 5-day mean; d = positions scaled by the full-sample sd; e = honest, filled at the next open.
