# Claim ledger: number-sense L1 to L3 (concept audit)

Blind audit of `app/content/courses/number-sense/` levels 1 to 3 (l1 to l14, review-1 to review-3, homework-1 to homework-3). One row per definition, result, method step, convention, always/never/every/only claim, interpretation, causal claim and the reasoning of each explain, why, hint and feedback line (lines that restate the same reasoning share a row). Paths are JSON paths inside the file. Verdicts: correct, wrong, imprecise, misleading, unverified. `sim:` names a check in `docs/reports/number-sense-L<n>-concepts.py`. A verdict of `imprecise` or `wrong` carries the finding number in `docs/reviews/audit-number-sense-1-3.md`.

Sources used: Courant and Robbins, *What is Mathematics?*, ch. I sec. 1 (laws of arithmetic), ch. II sec. 1 (rational numbers, negatives and the rule of signs from the distributive law), ch. II sec. 2 (decimal fractions, periodic decimals); Hardy and Wright, *An Introduction to the Theory of Numbers*, ch. IX (Theorem 135: a/b terminates exactly when b, in lowest terms, is 2^α 5^β; Theorem 136 on periods); Spivak, *Calculus*, ch. 1 (properties P1 to P12; (−a)(−b) = ab; a/0 undefined); Rudin, *Principles of Mathematical Analysis*, Theorem 1.21 (unique positive n-th root) and ch. 1 exercise 6 (laws of exponents); Gelfand and Shen, *Algebra*, the sections on powers, negative powers and fractions; Taylor, *An Introduction to Error Analysis*, sec. 2.2 (significant figures); Hull, *Options, Futures and Other Derivatives*, ch. 15 (volatility and the square-root-of-time rule under independent returns); Bacon, *Practical Portfolio Performance Measurement and Attribution*, ch. 4 (drawdown); ISO 80000-2 (mathematical signs: −a^n means −(a^n); order of operations); Python Language Reference sec. 6.5 (the power operator binds tighter than a unary minus on its left); Microsoft Support, "Calculation operators and precedence in Excel" (negation ranks above exponentiation); ECMAScript 2016, exponentiation operator (a unary minus on the base is a SyntaxError). Market figures (UK output, world equity value) are checked against the auditor's recall of ONS and World Federation of Exchanges totals to one significant figure, not against a live source.
