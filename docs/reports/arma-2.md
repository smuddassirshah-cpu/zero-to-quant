## Departures from the brief, with reasons

- **The brief's l8 trap claim does not hold on seed 5.** It says AIC and Ljung-Box agree that p = 1 is enough. In fact AIC is lowest at p = 3, by 1.65. The trap therefore teaches the honest version: the AIC gap is under 2, BIC prefers p = 1 by 6.8, and the AR(1) residuals pass. `aic_order` gained a `tol` argument: with `tol=0` it returns 3 on seed 5, which is tested; with `tol=2` it returns 1.
- **One lag the brief missed.** Lags 15 to 17 of seed 5's ACF lie outside the band. l5 uses them as the "chance bars" screen, and `lags_outside_band` expects [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 15, 16, 17].
- **Code screens moved or dropped.**
  - `simulate_ar1` is not repeated in l7: the learner wrote it in level 1 l2.
  - `ar1_moments` became a homework item (d3), and l7 instead fills the moments in a table.
  - `false_alarm_rate` uses the learner's `ljung_box_q`, NumPy and SciPy only, to keep the simulation fast.
- **The homework Ljung-Box differs from l6's.** The homework "from scratch" version uses only `math`, with the closed-form χ² tail for even m. This keeps it distinct from l6's SciPy version.
- **l6 hook layout.** A pair cannot hold a pair, so the four correlograms are two panels of two coloured blocks each, at lags 1 to 12.
