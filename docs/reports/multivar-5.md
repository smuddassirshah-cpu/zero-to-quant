## Departures from the brief, with reasons

- **The 10% target with a 50% cap is infeasible.** The brief asks for the 10% target in l20 and the 9% target with a 50% limit in the homework. With every weight at most 50% the best expected return is $(0, 0.5, 0.5) = 9.5\%$, so 10% cannot be reached. At 9% the free optimum has every weight under 0.4524, so a 50% cap never binds. l20's transfer therefore uses the long-only limit $w_i \ge 0$ at 10% (the boundary $w_1 = 0$, volatility 28.48%) and says in its explanation that a 50% cap would make 10% unreachable. The homework longform uses a 45% cap at 9%, where $w_3 = 0.4524$ breaks it and the answer moves to the boundary.
- **l19's hook drags a point in $(x, y)$, not along a trace.** There is no 3D canvas, so the plane $x + 2y + 3z = 14$ is parametrised by $(x, y)$ with $z$ following, and the learner drags P over contours of $x^2 + y^2 + z^2$ restricted to the plane. The next screen is the three-variable Lagrange solution.
- **l17's box method uses six pieces, not four triangles.** For edges $(a, b)$ and $(c, d)$ from the origin, the box minus the parallelogram is two triangles of legs $a, b$, two of legs $c, d$ and two $c$ by $b$ rectangles. The derive screen says so and gets $ad - bc$.
- **l18 puts the 3D bell in the derive screen** (product route and spherical check, in order) rather than a separate ladder screen, and adds a prediction screen (larger, smaller or the same) before the transfer. The edge case of a solid with a split shadow (the tent) is in the quiz.
- **Quiz and ladder counts.** l19 has six ladder-type screens; the validator allows it. l20's first two screens are `problem` screens (the hook is a tap).
