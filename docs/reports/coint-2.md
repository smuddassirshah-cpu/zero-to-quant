## Generators, seeds and conventions

- **Level 1's generators**, verbatim in every prelude: `make_two_walks`, `make_pair`, `make_broken_pair` and `make_case` (lesson 2's pairs A to D), as level 1 wrote them.
- **Pair C** is `make_pair(3, 1000)`; `make_pair(3, 250)` equals its first 250 days (checked); `make_pair(3, 1500)` is used for the rolling hedge and the rolling z.
- **l8** (in its prelude, `gens8.py`):
  - `make_three(seed=5)` exactly as the brief defines it: $U_0 = 0$, $U_t = 0.8U_{t-1} + 0.5e_{t,1:3}$, series $w + U_0$, $0.8w + U_1$, $1.2w + U_2$.
  - `make_two_trends(seed=6)`: two walks $w_1, w_2$ and series $w_1 + U_0$, $w_2 + U_1$, $w_1 + w_2 + U_2$, rank 1.
  - `make_three_walks(seed=7)`: three independent walks, rank 0.
- **l6 volatility edge case**: `default_rng(11)`, AR(1) with $\phi = 0.9$, shock sd 0.5 on days 0 to 499 and 1.0 after.
- **l7 hook**: two AR(1)s with half-lives 5 and 60 days ($\phi = 0.5^{1/H}$), long-run sd 1 (shock sd $\sqrt{1 - \phi^2}$), the same shocks from `default_rng(21)`, 250 days.
- **Rolling conventions**:
  - Every window ends today and includes it; sds are sample sds (ddof 1); pandas `rolling(w)`, as `fin-ml` l5.
  - `rolling_hedge` and `residual_z` use `np.polyfit` on days $t - w + 1$ to $t$, as level 3's `rolling_fit`.
- **Half-life**: the regression $\Delta s_t = a + b s_{t-1}$, $h = \ln 0.5/\ln(1 + b)$, as `arma` l12.

## Departures from the brief, with reasons

- **The l5 trap's claim does not hold on the 250-day window.** The brief expected the inverted ($x$ on $y$) hedge to leave a less stationary spread there. In fact its Engle-Granger statistic is more negative: −3.50 ($p = 0.032$) against −3.19 ($p = 0.072$). The trap therefore targets the misconception itself, "the regression turned round is the same hedge". The slopes multiply to $R^2 = 0.85$. Its explanation reports the honest finding: on this window the test's verdict at 5% flips with the direction. Carried forward 750 days, the 2.13 hedge leaves a spread sd of £10.90 against £5.60.
- **`trace_table` rounds critical values to 4 places, not 3.** Python's `round(3.8415, 3)` is 3.841, which would contradict the 3.842 a learner expects. Four places reproduces the tabulated values exactly, and the trace statistics stay at 3.
- **New generators.** The brief asks for a planted two-trend case and three independent walks; `make_two_trends(6)` and `make_three_walks(7)` are mine and sit in l8's prelude. l6's changing-volatility spread and l7's hook spreads are also mine; seeds are listed above.
- **Code screens merged to fit 12 screens.** `hedge_ratio` and `both_directions` share a screen, as do `full_sample_z` and `lookahead_days`, and `johansen_rank` and `trace_table`. `normalise_vector` raises ValueError on a zero first entry, a case the brief left open.
- **The l6 trap gives the level 3 numbers now.** They were computed here with the brief's reference backtest: Sharpe 1.124 against 1.647.
- **`spread_report`'s z.** Today's z uses the full-sample OLS residual. On the last day the full sample contains no future, so the explanation says this is honest there and only there.
