## Seeds and conventions

- £ throughout, contracts at £1 a point; pence only in the microstructure links (500p, 400p).
- Ledger columns are Trade, Price (£), Position and P&L at fair (£): four narrow columns that fit at 390 px.
- Python seeds: 5 (l6 random counterparty, l7 trades), 6 (l6 P&L path), 4 (l8 rolls), 139 (l9 deal), 371 (homework mock deal), 1, 2, 3 and 7 (homework `make_script`), and 8 (simulation check). The app's own sim seed is 6 (l6 explore).
- Quotes on game screens are graded by `sliders` `check` products with `value: 1`. Code screens are pure standard-library Python; exact answers are `Fraction`s with `prelude: "from fractions import Fraction\n"`.
- Every scripted game is a fixed sequence of counterparty actions, one per screen or event, so it can move to the trading-game widget (E12) without changing its numbers.
