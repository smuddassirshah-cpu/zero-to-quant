# Concept audit: recruiting, levels 1 to 3

Blind concept audit (brief: `docs/briefs/concept-audit.md`) of `app/content/courses/recruiting/` levels 1 to 3: l1 to l15, review-1 to review-3, homework-1 to homework-3. Stage 0 depth. Every screen, quiz item, review item, homework prompt, model and rubric was read, with every graded key, wrong option and slip. The ledger is `docs/reports/audit-recruiting-1-3-ledger.md` (1,077 rows). Arithmetic, dates, probabilities and the two empirical claims are recomputed in `docs/reports/recruiting-L1-concepts.py`, `-L2-` and `-L3-`. The scripts read the graded keys from the content, so they pass once a fix lands.

The audit had no web access, so facts about named firms, dates and pay could not be checked at source. Where the content labels such a fact "as reported" and tells the learner to check the firm's page, the ledger marks it `unverified` and names what would settle it; the list is under "Unverified claims" below. Audit date: 10 November 2026.

Findings follow the pattern `N. file | JSON path | severity, tag | problem | evidence and basis | exact fix`. Every fix is paste-ready text in the course voice. Paths use `screens[n]`, `quiz[n]`, `items[n]`.

Totals: 19 findings. `wrong` 1 (minor). `imprecise` 17 (2 serious, 15 minor). `misleading` 1 (minor).

## Advice checked for harm

The brief asks for a view on advice that could hurt a learner. I found none that is unsafe.

- Reneging (l14 `screens[4]`, `[6]`, `quiz[3]`; homework-3 `items[7]`): the course says accepting is a promise, withdraw from other processes, and do not accept as a hedge. That is the standard careers-service position and the cautious one. It claims no legal bar, and it hedges the university consequence with "at some universities".
- Deadlines (l14 `screens[0]` to `[2]`, `[10]`): tell the other firm at once, ask once for an extension with a reason and a date, decide in time. Sound.
- Honesty (l5 `screens[6]`, l6 `screens[6]`, l8 `screens[7]`, l10 `screens[8]`, l13 `screens[7]`): never misstate a class year, pad skills or invent a fact or story. Sound.
- Right to work (l2 `screens[5]`, `[9]`; l14 `screens[8]`): check it early and ask the recruiter; no specific visa rule is asserted. Sound.
- Assessments (l9 `screens[8]`, `[9]`): do not use the real link as practice; report a fault at once and do not restart unasked. Sound.
