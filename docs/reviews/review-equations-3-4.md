# Equations levels 3 and 4 (Inequalities and formulae; Simultaneous equations): second-reader findings

Scope: `app/content/courses/equations/` l10 to l17, `review-3.json`, `review-4.json`, `homework-3.json`, `homework-4.json`. Continuity skim of L1 and L2 (first screens, summaries, every bolded term and every setup L3 and L4 reuse), and of the courses that require equations L3 (functions L4, quadratics L3). This is Stage 0, for a GCSE-entry learner, so every step was also read for knowledge not yet taught and for conventions that differ from UK GCSE usage.

There is no `docs/briefs/equations.md` (or `gap-equations.md`) and no author report under `docs/reports/`: the course predates the brief and report scheme. The levels were read against `course.json` (level maps, `lessonObjectives`, `requires`) and AUTHORING.

Method: every graded key, every `wrong[]` value and every number in prose, hints, feedback, explanations, canvases and model answers was recomputed in two new scripts (`docs/reports/equations-L3-numbers.py`, `-L4-numbers.py`, on `numbers_lib`). Each pair of simultaneous equations is solved two ways, by Cramer's rule in exact fractions and then by substitution back into both equations. Each inequality is checked by brute force over integers or quarters as well as by the algebra. Every `wrong[]` value was rebuilt from the slip its `why` or `byKey` names, and every `why` was read against the slip it claims. Claims that are not single numbers (which side a bar runs, what a slip produces, a "holds $b - a$" count) are `claim()` or `stated()` checks. These levels have no code items. The single `expression` item (l16 `screens[8]`) was graded with the app's own `grade.js` and Compute Engine 0.30.2 in a node harness, on the forms a learner would type and on the listed slips. The engine was read for how `tap-regions`, `place`, `order`, `multi`, `drag-point` and `manipulate` pick their keys, so every `byKey` line was checked against what can actually fire.
