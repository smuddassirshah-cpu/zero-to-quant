Method. Each listed item was read whole. `recompute.py` recomputes every changed key, band, `wrong` value and printed number from the item's own prose, with a second route (fractions, a scan of the payoff, a midpoint integral, Black-Scholes at the implied vol, finite differences, a tree, seeded simulations for the four claims in the text-only items), runs the code of the reasoning items, and searches each re-authored item for numbers of its old version. `grade.mjs` puts some 380 typed strings through the engine's `numGrade` on 38 items: other right forms, the value the feedback prints, the unrounded slip, the answer one step short, a minus sign and a £ sign. Code tasks: I wrote a solution to each of the 14 from `statement.py` (prose, question, starter, examples) before reading the model or the tests (`mine.py`); `code_run.py` runs model, mine, starter and 147 candidate-wrong solutions (`cands.py`, 10 to 12 a task, 6 for `binom_tail`) on every example and hidden test; `pyo_time.mjs` times the borderline ones in Pyodide 0.27.7 under node against the 5 s limit. Freshness was read, not left to `fresh-check`: `find.py` searches every content file for the smallest screen, part or item that holds all of an item's numbers or words (`fresh-probes.json`, plus ad hoc probes), and I read what it pointed at; all 62 behavioural models were read side by side. `proposed.py` and `proposed_code.py` compute the numbers and tests of every fix below, twice.

Scripts: `docs/reviews/review-delta-bank-2-scripts/`, run from the repo root with the scratchpad venv Python: `recompute.py`, `proposed.py`, `proposed_code.py`, `code_run.py [id ...]`, `find.py [probes.json | -e <re> -e <re> ...]`; `node grade.mjs [--show]`; `node pyo_time.mjs <pkgs>/pyodide-0.27.7/package`; reading aids `show.py`, `brief.py`, `statement.py <id> ...`. `mine.py` holds my solutions, `cands.py` the candidates, `code_run_lib.py` the judge (the rule of `__zq_judge` in `widgets/code.js`), `fresh-probes.json` the freshness probes.
