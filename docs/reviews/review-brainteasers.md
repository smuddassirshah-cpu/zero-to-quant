# Topic 3.3 (brainteasers): second-reader findings

Scope: every lesson, review and homework file in `app/content/courses/brainteasers/` (l1 to l16, review-1 to review-3, homework-1 to homework-3; 22 files, 192 lesson screens, 55 quiz items, 29 review items, 24 homework items).
Method: every stated number and every puzzle solution recomputed in Python, by exhaustive search or enumeration wherever the puzzle allows it (scripts in the scratchpad folder `read-brainteasers/`):
- `v_l1.py`: lizard states by BFS (9 reachable from 1, 2, 3), lamp patterns over GF(2) (32 on 3 × 3, 128 on 4 × 4), coin-turning BFS, the board invariants, the `manipulate` state machine.
- `v_l2.py`, `v_lvl1.py`: tilings by exact cover (5 × 5 and 7 × 7 minus one, 1 × 3 on 7 × 7, 1 × 4 and T on 6 × 6, T on 4 × 7), all 824 Hamiltonian paths of 5 × 5, closed tours, the 27 cube paths, knight BFS, colour counts.
- `v_l4.py` / `v_l4b.py`: a full counterfeit-coin solver over (either-way, heavy-only, light-only, genuine-available) states, for "find and say which" and for "find only".
- `v_l5.py`: jug BFS for every pair asked, the no-tap 10/7/3 BFS, both `manipulate` machines checked transition by transition.
- `v_l8.py`: all permutations for 4, 6 and 8 prisoners; exact harmonic tails for 10, 12, 20 and 100.
- `v_l11.py`: Dijkstra over every bridge plan for each set of times, and BFS for the auditors-and-traders crossing; the l11 bridge `manipulate` (46 states) checked.
- `v_lvl2.py`: the homework-2 code item (starter fails 7 of 8 tests, solution passes all 8), and minimax egg drop by brute force.
- Inline checks: clocks, rates, digits, factorial zeros, telescoping, constants and the rule of 72 against `math`.

Mechanical scans (`mech.py`): `shuffle` on every choice and multi, `tags` and `pace` on every quiz, review and homework item, tolerance overlaps between key and `wrong` values, every `wrong` and distractor id against `byKey`, firm names, `!`, em and en dashes, American spellings, unbalanced `$`. `docs/reviews/voice-scan.py` run into `docs/reviews/voice-3.3-brainteasers.txt`.

Format: `path | screen/item | severity | what is wrong | fix`. Severity: error = wrong number, false claim, wrong grading or a puzzle with two defensible readings; warning = weak explanation, mismatched feedback or teaching issue; note = style, layout or wording.

**Counts: 3 errors, 8 warnings, 19 notes (voice scan 0 and 0, so no voice notes).**

Checked and clean: every keyed answer in the course except the three errors below; every `wrong` value matches a real slip except where listed; no tolerance overlaps; `tags` and `pace` on all 108 quiz, review and homework items and on every drill screen; no firm names (the three books are named once per level, in l1, l6 and l12, as the brief asks); no `!`, no em or en dashes, no American spellings, £ for money throughout. Puzzles are classic families written in the course's own words; where a canonical instance is kept (12 coins, 100 prisoners and 50 boxes, 100 floors, three and three on a river) the brief prescribed it.
