### Delta read: what holds

- `bisect`: root at either end returned with 2 calls; `tol` of 1e-17, 0 or −1 stops within one float gap in under 60 calls; same-sign values near ±1e-200 raise `ValueError`; an exact zero at a midpoint is still found; error at most tol/2; 42 calls on x² − 2.
- `safe_newton`: the rule "Newton only if inside the bracket and at most half the step before last" is the Numerical Recipes `rtsafe` rule and is sound. Root at an end, missing sign change, the Newton cycle on x³ − 2x + 2, a zero slope at the start, the ninefold root with `ftol=0`, `xtol` below a float gap and the `RuntimeError` at the cap all behave as the docstring says. 3,000 random problems solve in at most 23 calls.
- `gl_call_price` / `gl_put_price`: strikes from 20 to 1,000 over four maturity and volatility pairs agree with Black-Scholes to 1.2e-11 at 64 nodes; a kink beyond ±8 is clipped and the empty side integrates to 0.
- `ytm_book`: the bracket test, the NaN rule and the safeguarded step are sound. The solution starts a zero-coupon bond at 0.05 where the prose says "starting at c"; it changes no result.
