Scope: every lesson, review and homework file in `app/content/courses/stat-learning/` (l1 to l14), `validation/` (l1 to l12), `fin-ml/` (l1 to l12) and `ensembles/` (l1 to l12), with review-1 to review-3 and homework-1 to homework-3 of each, and `app/content/exams/topic-2.1.json`.

Method. Every hand-computable number was recomputed in Python and checked a second way (by hand or by a second formula): MSEs, bias-variance sums, Gini and entropy gains, squared-error drops, ridge and lasso shrinkage, odds, log-odds and sigmoids, precision, recall and AUC by pair counts, Brier and Kelly growth, hit-rate standard errors and $t$ statistics, `TimeSeriesSplit` boundaries, purge counts, uniqueness weights, fractional-differencing weights, the PSR, $E[\max]$ and DSR (Bailey and López de Prado's closed form, and the exact normal maximum by numerical integration), AFML bet sizes, costs, break-even costs and capacity. Seeded numbers quoted in prose were spot-checked by loading each widget's prelude and solution into CPython with NumPy 2.5, pandas 3.0 and scikit-learn 1.6.1 (scratchpad `ns.py`): l1 and l2 of stat-learning, l5's hook (−0.919 against −0.953, below), validation l1's split statistics, `KFold` folds and the hypergeometric fold chances, and others named in the findings. Code: all 182 code widgets were run in a CPython harness that mirrors `code.js` (prelude, solution or starter, postlude, every hidden test with its `compare` and `tol`; scratchpad `runcode.py`). Every solution passes every hidden test; every starter fails at least one (22 starters pass some tests, none pass all). Grading: every numeric `wrong[]` was checked against the answer's tolerance, every choice and tap answer against its prose. Style: a script (scratchpad `style.py`) over every string outside code, KaTeX and data. Layout: `node tools/playthrough.mjs` with `--local-cdn` over all 50 lessons, 24 sets and the exam (all passed, 1,126 screenshots), and a `--mobile` run of validation l5 and l6, fin-ml l4 and l12, ensembles l7 and l10, stat-learning l11 and the exam (all passed); about 20 screenshots were looked at, chosen for graph, tree, pair, grid, plane and wide-table canvases. `validate.mjs` reports 0 errors (27 warnings for these courses: 22 canvas-reuse, 4 point-hint, 1 canvas-less problem).

Format: `path | screen/item | severity | what is wrong | fix`. Severity: error = wrong number, false claim or wrong grading; warning = weak or contradictory explanation, grading that accepts a named slip or rejects a defensible answer, a binding-rule breach, or a broken layout; note = style or small polish. Screen, item and quiz numbers are zero-based.
