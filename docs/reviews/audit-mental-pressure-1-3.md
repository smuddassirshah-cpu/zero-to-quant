# Concept audit: mental-pressure, levels 1 to 3

Blind concept audit (brief: `docs/briefs/concept-audit.md`) of `app/content/courses/mental-pressure/` levels 1 to 3: l1 to l12, review-1 to review-3, homework-1 to homework-3. Claim ledger: `docs/reports/audit-mental-pressure-1-3-ledger.md` (260 rows: correct 240, imprecise 10, unverified 7, wrong 0, misleading 0, with three dual-verdict rows counted under their first verdict). Checks: `docs/reports/mental-pressure-L1-concepts.py`, `-L2-concepts.py`, `-L3-concepts.py`. Arithmetic is left to the number scripts; this audit covers meaning: definitions, methods, conventions, bounds and the reasoning in explain, why, hint and feedback lines.

Findings are `N. file | JSON path | severity | problem | evidence and basis | exact fix`. Severities: `wrong`, `imprecise`, `misleading`. No `wrong` and no `misleading` finding was made; every finding is `imprecise` (true under a hypothesis or a reading the text drops).

## Checked and clean

Every identity the methods rest on holds as stated: cross multiplication over all 8,100 two-digit pairs, difference of squares, squares ending in 5, the factor-25 and factor-12 and factor-15 tricks, estimate-and-correct (n/d = q + (n − qd)/d, one digit per round, divmod over 10,000 pairs), places add and scale-both for decimals, a/b > c/d iff ad > bc with the hypothesis b, d > 0 stated on the generalise and derive screens and checked exhaustively to denominator 30, the gap (ad − bc)/bd, growth factors and the p² loss, the undo rules p/(1 + p) and p/(1 − p) with the −100% edge, points against percent (old-rate base stated), relative errors adding to first order with the cross term bounded at 0.25% at 5% each, the tangent above the concave root curve, the one-step rule n + d/(2n) always high by exactly h² with error about d²/(8n³) (within a quarter of it for every non-square to 10,000; "one place is usually safe" holds: 7.5% of the roots to 1,000 round differently at one decimal, and every root item's key accepts the taught step), the √252 and √12 volatility convention named as such, cheapest-first optimality (brute force on 2,000 cost sets), the guess values −0.5, −1/3, 0 (400,000 draws each), difference tables settling for every polynomial of degree 0 to 4 and never for a geometric sequence, every sequence key following its stated rule and that rule fitting every term shown (56 items), Moser's 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 31, the net score 2r − a, the three-type sort applied consistently to all 15 reviewed rows, the mock generator's 80 answers recomputed from their texts, and both longform models recomputed. No code solution teaches a wrong method: `bigger` is exact where floats tie, `sorted` stability is real, `next_by_differences` and `next_term` refuse what they should, `reverse_change` and `rate_change` return `None` where the division is undefined. No `wrong[]` value or distractor is correct under a valid alternative method (the alternatives offered, such as dividing by n or by n², the new-rate base, p/(1 + p) after a loss, are each wrong). The 80-in-8 format claims (80 questions, 8 minutes, four options, +1/−1, no skipping, a pass line of about 50 to 60 net) are stated as "reported" and are `unverified` in the ledger, as are the Zetamac default-setting claims the app's `warmup.json` copies; none is contradicted by anything in the session. There are no `manipulate` widgets and no generated drills in these three levels, so there was no prose-against-acceptance rule to check for them; the text-cell items (L or R, S, M or T, HH:MM) accept exactly the forms their prose names.
