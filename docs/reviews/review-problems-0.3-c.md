Method. The two authors' scripts were run first. Every part was then solved from its text before its key was read, by hand, and again in `problems-0.3-c-scripts/mine.py` by a route of my own: every series answer by direct summation or by stepping the sequence term by term in exact fractions (400 rises of the ball, 600 payments of plan B, the twelve present values of the gym added one by one, picks counted with `itertools`), trig by coordinates (the hexagon's corners, the lake with P at the origin, the lamp's elbow and bulb as points, the wheel tip as a complex number, grid scans for periods, peaks, crossings and time above a level), sympy for the exact forms.
