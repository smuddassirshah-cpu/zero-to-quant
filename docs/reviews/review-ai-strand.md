# Review: AI strand proposal (second read, 27 Nov 2026)

Checked against `curriculum.json`, every `course.json`, the lessons named, the engine files cited and AUTHORING sections 9 and 12 to 16. Corrected curriculum: `docs/briefs/ai-strand-curriculum.md`.

Verdict: right shape, wrong placement. Stage 1 teaches machine learning before 2.1 does, the engine cannot keep the strand off the quant path, and a third of the levels duplicate something.

## Findings

**1. Blocking. The engine cannot enforce "never gates".** `content.js` (`strandOfCourse`) accepts only `quant` and `pure`, and the validator rejects any other value (AUTHORING 9). "Quant path only" hides pure courses only. The stage count ("n of m topics passed") counts every topic. Today keeps stage order for Undecided. Role readiness needs every level of required courses (AUTHORING 15). `curriculum-hours.mjs` strips only `pure` from the quant-path figures. Change: engine item 0, `strand: "ai"` with its own visibility setting, kept out of stage counts, Today (unless opted in), quant-path hours, readiness and stage papers.

**2. Blocking. 1.12 teaches machine learning before 2.1 does.** `nn-train` L4 (weight decay, early stopping) needs `stat-learning` l2-training-error-lies, l7-shrinking and `validation` L1, all Stage 2. `makemore` L3 "held-out evaluation" needs the same. `nn-train` L1 (cross-entropy) comes before `stat-learning` l10-predicting-a-probability, which derives log loss from maximum likelihood. `nn-train` L2 precedes `convex` l14-gradient-descent (step size from Hessian eigenvalues). Change: `nn-train` moves to 2.7; 1.12 keeps autodiff and first language models. Held-out loss rests on `testing` L3 (out-of-sample Sharpe).

**3. Major. The Stage 0 exit uses calculus and duplicates Stage 1.** "Fits a line by gradient descent" needs a gradient (`multivar` L2). It is also `multivar` l8-walking-downhill's transfer screen (`fit_line` by gradient descent). `first-python` L1 teaches values only, with no loops. `counting` L1 has no probability (that is l8-counts-to-chances, L2). Change: the exit fits a line by nudging each weight and keeping changes that lower the error. Edges: `first-python`/L3, `counting`/L2, `functions`/L2, `number-sense`/L3.

**4. Major. The strand reteaches itself and the app.** Reteaching the app: `backprop` L1 "derivatives as nudges" (`limits` L2, `taylor` L1) and `nn-train` L1 (`estimation` L2, `stat-learning` l10). Repeated inside the strand: normalisation in `cnn` L4 and `nn-train` L3; scaling counts in `ai-papers` L3 and `gpt` L4; constitutional methods in `post-train` L3 and `ai-safety` L3; evaluations in `post-train` L4 and `ai-safety` L2; risks in `ai-use` L3 and `ai-safety` L1; prompting in `ai-use` L2 and `llm-apps` L1. Change: each idea has one home and later levels build on it (change log rows 4a to 4h).

**5. Major. Existing coverage was not read.** `stat-learning` l17-a-small-network already teaches XOR, ReLU, parameter counts (2-2-1 has 9), a NumPy forward pass, one chain-rule gradient step and `MLPClassifier`. l15-widest-street teaches SVMs with RBF kernels, and l16 teaches clustering. `eigen` l11 teaches principal components, `ensembles` l2-good-split teaches entropy (as impurity), and `graphs-dp` l17-dice-stopping teaches backward induction. Change: these become `requires` edges; no kernels or SVM course. `ai-basics` L3 must take fresh numbers, not l17's corners (`fresh-check.mjs`).

**6. Major. Information theory is missing but used.** A grep of all content finds no "Kullback", "KL", "cross-entropy" or "perplexity". The VAE, the RLHF KL penalty and DPO all need KL divergence. Change: new course `info` (2 levels) in 1.12, built on `random-vars` L5 (Jensen) and `estimation` L2.

**7. Major. Wrong, missing and forward edges.** 2.9 names `fin-ml` L1 (labels), but text leakage is L2 ("Features with hidden look-ahead"). `rl` lacks `limits-thm` L4 (Beta family, for Thompson sampling), `estimation` L3 (intervals, for UCB) and `numerics` L4 (control variates, which is what a policy-gradient baseline is). `rl` L5 search needs minimax, which `game-theory` L1 teaches in Stage 3.3: a forward edge, so L5 teaches it in place. Diffusion needs `eigen` L4 (the multivariate normal). The topological order needed for backprop is `graphs-dp` l5-tasks-in-order, whose closure drags in `ds` and `algo-think`. `momentum-rep` L1 drags in `alpha` L1 to L4 and `fin-ml` L3. Change: edges corrected; the two heavy ones are `gate: false` with a `why`.

**8. Major. The CNN exit does not test a CNN.** I ran scikit-learn 1.9.1 on digits (8 by 8, 70/30 stratified split, seeds 0 and 1). Accuracy: logistic regression 0.970 and 0.963, 3-NN 0.985 and 0.983, SVC 0.987 and 0.983, and a 64-unit MLP 0.978 and 0.974. A linear model clears 95%. My NumPy CNN (16 filters, max-pool, 2,730 parameters) reached 0.985. On test digits shifted one pixel it scored 0.643, against 0.483 for a 3,010-parameter MLP. Change: the exit is at least 98% held out and a clear margin over a same-size MLP on shifted digits. The level's number script sets the thresholds (my figures: one seed).

**9. Major. Training in the browser is under-specified.** Grading allows 5 s per case (`TEST_MS`, `code.js` line 28), plus a `budget` of at most 120,000 ms (`validate.mjs` lines 702, 794, 1365 to 1366), plus 30 s for heavy imports. The CNN above trains 40 epochs in 0.72 s natively, so it fits even at a large WASM slowdown. A tiny GPT does not fit as planned. Nothing in the proposal gives the learner an autograd, so "train it on Shakespeare" means hand-writing the backward pass of attention and layer norm. Change: `backprop` L4 builds a small array autograd that every later course reuses. Graded cases use supplied weights or short runs, and the long run happens outside the app. No lesson uses `load_digits` yet, so the spike must confirm that it works offline in Pyodide. Training pass marks are thresholds, not exact losses (Pyodide and the venv may sum floats differently).

**10. Major. `llm-apps` L1 and L3 cannot be graded.** The app has no language model to prompt or to call tools from. Change: `llm-apps` keeps two gradeable levels (retrieval maths, LLMs in quant research). Prompting and agents move outside; a self-marked write-up cannot meet a level (AUTHORING 13).

**11. Major. `ml-int` placed in 3.5 breaks topic mechanics.** A topic shows a strand badge only when all its courses share one (AUTHORING 9). Passing `topic-3.5` would test out `ml-int`. Folding it into `research-int` would make it required for researcher readiness. Overlap check against `research-int`: L2 shares only bias and variance with `ml-int` L1, while L3 "The open case" matches `ml-int` L3 in method. That is under half, so it stays a separate course. Change: `ml-int` moves to 3.6; its L3 becomes a deep learning case on top of `research-int` L3.

**12. Major. Topics missing at the depth claimed.** Added where they earn their hours: GPU and precision arithmetic (`gpt` L5, on `numerics` L1), experiment hygiene with seeds, ablations and paired comparisons (`nn-train` L4, on `testing` L2), graph networks (`representations` L5, on `markov` L4 "Random walks on networks"), and a VAE and diffusion split into a level each. Not added: kernels and SVMs (finding 5); MLOps serving and monitoring (not practisable in-app; drift is `validation` L2).

**13. Minor. Five of the eight canvases exist.** `graph` already draws l17's network, with edge weights, `state.values` and `frames` stepping. That covers `network` and `compgraph`. `plane` has `contour`, `heat` and polylines (`multivar` l8 and `convex` l14 draw descent paths). `matrix` highlight with frames covers `kernel`. `array` ranges and `steps` cover `tokens`. `grid` paths with arrows cover most of `gridworld`. The real gap is a value-coloured matrix. Change: drop five canvases; extend `graph`, `matrix` and `grid`.

**14. Minor. Resources.** Google ML Crash Course assumes algebra, Python and pandas, so it is dropped from Stage 0. BlueDot's "AI Safety Fundamentals" now runs as "Technical AI Safety" (bluedot.org). CS336 lectures are free, but the course site points self-learners to paid cloud GPUs for the assignments. Jumper et al. 2021 is used by no level and is dropped. Kaplan et al. 2020 and Bengio et al. 2003 are named in levels but were missing, so they are added. ARENA needs PyTorch and a GPU: outside only. The DeepMind x UCL RL series has 13 lectures (checked). UDL chapters 13, 17 and 18 were checked; the others are unverified. The Elements of AI hours are unverified.

**15. Minor. `interp` L4 is out of reach.** Attribution graphs need transcoders trained on a production model. Change: L4 is ablation and patching on the app's tiny models; attribution graphs are reading only.

**16. Minor. Dataset licences.** Digits is CC BY 4.0 at UCI (dataset 80) and needs a credit line. `names.txt` (karpathy/makemore, SSA data) and Tiny Shakespeare (karpathy/char-rnn) are public-domain text in repositories said to be MIT: unverified, check at build.

**17. Minor. Totals.** The proposal's topic figures sum to 126 h in the app, not 127. Its 980 h would add 26% to the 3,736 stored hours of the whole path. Revised: 950 h stored, 126 h in the app and 824 h outside, about 14.6 months at 15 h a week (each figure computed twice in the curriculum file). Headline totals must show the strand apart.

**18. Minor. Progression.** "After 1.3, 1.4, 1.6" puts 1.12 about two years after 0.8. Change: 1.12 opens when its edges are met, and runs alongside 1.8. Stage 1 and later levels need a write, build or derive objective (`perf-rules.mjs`). Topic exams hold no training runs. The strand final needs an exam kind outside topics and stages.
