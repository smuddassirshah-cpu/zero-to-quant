# Problem sets 2.1, validation levels 1 to 3: second-reader findings

Scope: `app/content/courses/validation/problems-1.json`, `-2`, `-3`: 12 problems, 55 parts (50 numeric, 5 choice), 109 `wrong` entries. Rules: `docs/briefs/chained.md`, `docs/briefs/chained-prompts.md` (Author rules). Pattern: `review-problems-analysis-1-3.md`.

Method. The author's script was run first. Lessons l1 to l12, `review-1` to `-3` and `homework-1` to `-3` were dumped to learner-facing text and read for what each level teaches and for reused settings, numbers and sentences. Every part was solved from its text and again in `docs/reports/problems-validation-numbers-read.py`, which imports nothing from the author's script. Its second routes: scikit-learn's own `KFold` and `TimeSeriesSplit` for every fold size and day number; a counting estimator inside a real `GridSearchCV` and nested `cross_val_score` for the 109, 436 and 400; brute sets of label days and feature days for every purge and embargo count; `scipy` for the hypergeometric, the chi-square sd and the Bonferroni bars; numerical integration for the mean best of 4, 16, 18, 22 and 60 normals; `statsmodels.acf`, `StandardScaler` and `pandas.rolling` run on the data; seeded simulation for the empty-fold chance, the correlation $1/7$, the sign-agreement rate, the best noise Sharpe of 18 over 9 years and the correlated best of 60. Every `wrong` value was rebuilt from the slip its line names, typed as the question asks (rounded, and as a per cent in a `%` field) and passed through the engine's rule ($|v - \text{key}| \le \text{abs} + 10^{-9}$, key first, then `wrong[]` in order). Claims were read beside the numbers. Reuse was read by eye and with `review-problems-validation-1-3-scripts/shared.py`, which lists every run of 7 or more words a set shares with a lesson, review or homework of the course, numbers masked, in every field. `resplit.py` simulates finding 2 and `proposed.py` computes the numbers of the fixes.

Severities: `wrong` (a learner is told a false number or marked wrongly), `misleading`, `reuse`, `voice`, `continuity`.

## Engine

1. `fresh-check.mjs` skips `feedback`, `explain`, `why` and option text (`SKIP_KEYS`), so a set that replays a lesson screen with new numbers and the lesson's own feedback lines shows 0 hits. Here 56 of 79 shared runs of 7 or more words sit in those fields, among them a 13-word sentence shared with l11. Same-course reuse was the commonest finding of session AC, and this is the part of it the tool cannot see. Suggest an advisory over those fields with numbers masked, and a count per item.
2. A story replayed with new numbers (the l8, l10, l11 and l12 cases) leaves no number or long stem run in common. Suggest comparing stems with numbers masked at a shorter run (6 words) for same-course lessons, as an advisory.
3. In a `%` field a learner who types the fraction (0.086 for 8.6%) is compared as 0.00086 and gets the generic line. Suggest a hint when 100 times the typed value is in the key band.
