# Concept audit: odes, levels 1 to 3

Blind concept audit (brief: `docs/briefs/concept-audit.md`) of `app/content/courses/odes/` levels 1 to 3: l1 to l13, review-1 to review-3, homework-1 to homework-3. Claim ledger: `docs/reports/audit-odes-1-3-ledger.md` (207 rows: correct 188, imprecise 16, wrong 2, misleading 1, unverified 0). Checks: `docs/reports/odes-L1-concepts.py`, `-L2-concepts.py`, `-L3-concepts.py` (sympy, numerical integration, Monte Carlo). Arithmetic is left to the author's number scripts; this audit covers meaning: definitions, theorems and their hypotheses, methods, conventions, always/never claims, graded keys, `wrong[]` options, and the reasoning in explain, why, hint and feedback lines.

Findings are `N. file | JSON path | severity | problem | evidence and basis | exact fix`. Severities: `wrong`, `imprecise`, `misleading`. Each is also tagged `serious` (a learner would carry away a wrong definition, theorem, method, convention or graded answer) or `minor` (wording or precision that changes no understanding). There are 14 findings: 2 `wrong`, 11 `imprecise`, 1 `misleading`. Four are `serious` (findings 2, 5, 6, 10). No graded key is wrong, and no `wrong[]` option or slip value is correct under a valid alternative method. One option graded true (finding 9) and one keyed "select all" option (finding 13) are true only under a hypothesis the item does not state.

BDP is Boyce and DiPrima, *Elementary Differential Equations and Boundary Value Problems*. Strogatz is *Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos*.

## Noted, not conceptual

- l5-mean-reversion.json screens[8]: `time_to_reach(r0, kappa, theta, target)` raises when `r0 == theta`, even for `target == theta`, where the time is 0. No test or text depends on it.

## Checked and clean

Definitions are the standard ones: differential equation, solution on an interval, order, separable, integrating factor, autonomous, equilibrium, phase line, semi-stable, mean reversion and half-life, second-order linear, homogeneous, boundary value problem, Wronskian, characteristic equation, particular solution, undetermined coefficients (apart from finding 6), under-, over- and critical damping, damping ratio, resonance, companion system, defective matrix, phase plane, saddle, node, spiral, centre, trace and determinant, Jacobian.

The domain points the brief asked for were each probed. Division by zero in separable equations is handled: the method screen states $h(y) \ne 0$, restores the constant solutions and tests it on the logistic. Interval of validity is taught with $1/(1 - t)$ and $\tan t$. The mean-reversion sign convention is $\kappa(\theta - r)$ throughout, the wrong sign is a taught trap, and the half-life $\ln 2/\kappa$ acts on the gap, with $1/\kappa$ named as the time to $1/e$. Units are consistent: $\kappa$ per year, half-lives in years, 252 trading days stated. Repeated and complex roots give the right forms, with the reason each works. Resonance is right on both counts: the undamped exact case grows as $\tfrac{t}{4}\sin 2t$, and the damped peak is put at $\omega_0\sqrt{1 - 2\zeta^2}$, below the natural frequency, with the natural frequency set as a `wrong` region. "More damping is always faster" is a taught trap, and critical damping is claimed only as fastest without overshoot. Classification by trace and determinant agrees with the eigenvalues on 20,000 integer matrices. The turning-direction rule holds on 2,000 random matrices with complex eigenvalues. The zero eigenvalue and the centre are both named as cases the linearisation cannot decide, and the Hartman-Grobman condition is stated correctly in quiz[2]. The hyperbolic claims "trusted for the nonlinear system" in review-3 items[6] and homework-3 items[4] were confirmed by simulating the nonlinear systems.
