## The orchestrator's four points

- ito L3 and the forward price: taught by level 3. ito l8 screen 10 (level 2) asks for the one-year forward price $100e^{0.05} = 105.13$ as the expected price under the pricing measure, and trees l10 (a gated prerequisite of ito L1) uses the share's forward price. The prompt also defines $F_t = S_te^{r(T_F - t)}$. Not a Must; the report's "taught in l16" is wrong, as l16 is level 4 (IT3-2).
- ito L4 with a path-dependent drift: valid under exactly the stated conditions. With $\mu_t$ adapted and $\int_0^T|\mu_t|\,dt < \infty$, $S$ is an Itô process, the lemma holds, and the cancellation $-\mu_tSV_S + \mu_tS\Delta = 0$ at $\Delta = V_S$ is pathwise. The seeded hedges agree. One gap of precision: no arbitrage is itself a condition on $\mu_t$ (IT4-3).
- alpha L3 "about 1.2": right as stated. $9.5/\sqrt{63} = 1.197$; with end effects $9.5/\sqrt{61.25} = 1.21$; counting that $s^2$ runs low by about 8% on overlapping data gives 1.16; the reader's seeded median gives 1.15 and the author's 1.13. The model states both approximations ($n$ far above $h$, $s$ close to the spread of one IC), every route is below 2, and the verdict does not change. No fix. The Newey-West line is the fault (AL3-2).
- opt-dash L3 and NaN: graded as above; a number, `None` or an exception fails four tests.
