## Method

The author's script was run first. Every part was then solved from its text and again in `docs/reports/problems-measure-prob-numbers-read.py`, which imports nothing from the author's script: the fat Cantor intervals built in exact fractions for stages 1 to 8; sigma-algebras by closure on bit masks; the law of $\lfloor 1/U \rfloor$ on an exact grid; all digit strings to length 5 and the recursion to 200 digits; $E[\lfloor X \rfloor]$ as $\sum k P(\lfloor X \rfloor = k)$, a route that avoids the tail sum; normal moments by sympy integrals; every mean of level 3 by enumeration (6 scenarios, 8 and 16 paths, 35 card orders), with the martingale property checked at every node. Each `wrong` value is rebuilt from the slip its line names. Lessons l1 to l13, `review-1` to `-3`, `homework-1` to `-3` and `exams/topic-3.2.json` were read for wording and reuse; `app/content` was grepped for each story and function. A probe on the engine's own `numGrade` and `exprGrade` (scratch `read-measure-prob/probe.mjs`) typed each key and each slip in the form asked, plus 44 strings of my own.
