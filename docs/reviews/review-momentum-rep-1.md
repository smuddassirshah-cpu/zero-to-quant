## Engine

- Validator `[shuffled-position]` (ENGINE-BACKLOG 585): `posExcluded` drops a hit when the place word appears anywhere on the screen, including inside a hyphenated compound. l4 `screens[8].hints[2]` "The first answer names all three" passed because the canvas holds "first-year" (`PLACE_ANY` matches `\bfirst\b` in "first-year"). The exclusion should ignore hyphenated compounds (`\bfirst\b(?!-)`), or require the place word near "row", "line", "step" or "column" in the screen context.
- `fresh-check.mjs --file` (set items): an item whose graded answer is stated as a fact in a lesson screen of the same course is answerable by recall, but nothing flags it. Review-1 `items[5]` asks for the 12/12 t that l3 `screens[3]` and `quiz[3]` print, 1.77. The question-side n-gram and shared-numbers checks do not see it, because the shared number is the answer. A check could compare each numeric `answer` (at its asked rounding) with the numbers in the same course's lesson prose and questions.
