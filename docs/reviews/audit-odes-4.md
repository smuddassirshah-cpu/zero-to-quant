Summary of the level: the mathematics and the numerical analysis are sound. Every transform, inverse, residue and solution of an initial value problem was checked against quadrature and an ODE solver. Every numerical-method claim was executed: Euler's first order (fitted 0.964 on the lesson's four steps, 0.9993 at small h), the midpoint method's second order, RK4's fourth order (ratio 15.35, fitted 3.97), the slipped RK4's order 1.979, the stability limits 2 and 2.7853 (root of the stability polynomial, and by simulation each side of it), the spiral radii 1.3681 and 0.9999996, the 163 and 4 steps of the bond, the 256 of the doubling search, the 16,000 Euler steps of the oscillator, and the convexity factor of the noisy bond (closed form and Monte Carlo). All hold. The one statement that would teach something false is in lesson 18: that an error inside the band of half a unit makes the printed four decimals agree. Two more `wrong` findings are console transcripts whose last digits the taught code does not print.
