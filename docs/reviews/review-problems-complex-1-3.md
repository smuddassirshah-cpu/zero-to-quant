# Problem sets 1.11, complex levels 1 to 3: second-reader findings

Scope: `app/content/courses/complex/problems-1.json`, `-2`, `-3`: 12 problems, 60 parts (32 numeric, 26 `expression`, 2 choice), 152 `wrong` entries. Rules: `docs/briefs/chained.md`. Pattern: `review-problems-1.4-c.md`. `l13-fourier-pricing` screens[10] was not judged (known open item); no part leans on it.

Method. The author's script was run first. The 13 lessons, three reviews, three homework sets, both fluency sets and exam `topic-1.11` were then skimmed screen by screen (`taught.py`), for what each level teaches and for reused settings and numbers. Every part was read whole (`show.py`), solved by hand from its text and again in `mine.py`, which imports nothing from the author's script and types every setting from the prose. Route 1 is exact sympy: `solve`, `arg` (asserted inside $(-\pi, \pi]$, the range l2 screens[2] defines), `residue`, exact powers, symbolic integrals and derivatives. Route 2 is floating point by another method: `cmath` modulus, phase, powers and logarithm, `numpy.roots`, central differences, the shoelace area of a mapped square and of the island, a line integral for the harmonic conjugate, the trapezium rule along and round contours, `scipy.integrate.quad` (with a cosine weight for the Cauchy characteristic function, a lognormal density for the share's moments and Black-Scholes prices over log strike for $\psi(0)$), and simulation (the lighthouse's flashes and their mean, the two print stages). Every key, every `accept` form and every `wrong` value or `wrong[].tex` was rebuilt from the slip its line names, and each number printed in a `feedback`, `byKey`, `why` or `explain` string was recomputed while reading. Every typed part then went through the engine's own graders in node with the local Compute Engine (`grade-probe.mjs`: `numGrade` and `exprGrade`): the key, with one more place, after a letter and `=`, with a true minus sign and with its unit; each `accept` form; each `wrong` entry on its own key; and 258 strings of my own in `rows.json` (other ways to write $p + qi$, the decimal value, the exact value in a decimal part, the angle in degrees, honest and dishonest roundings, units, the commonest slip, the answer one step short). `try-fix.mjs` tries the content fixes below on in-memory copies with the same graders, and `proposed.py` computes their numbers twice. Freshness was read by eye, helped by `fresh.py` (key numbers, expressions and nouns of each problem across `app/content`).

Tags: `wrong` = a learner is marked wrongly or told something false in the content; `unclear` = the question can be read two ways, does not say the form wanted, or refuses a sensible entry; `minor` = weak part, loose `wrong` entry, missing restatement, wording or freshness.
