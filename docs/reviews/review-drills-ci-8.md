# Drills pack `ci`, pack 8 (intervals, greedy, bits, simulation): second-reader findings

Scope: the 15 files `app/content/drills/ci-*.json` tagged `pack-8`, at commit 2af4b08, with `docs/reports/drills-ci-8.md`, `drills-ci-8-check.py`, `drills-ci-8-indep.py` and `drills-ci-8-wrong.py`, against `docs/briefs/drills-ci-packs-5-8.md`, `drills-ci-packs.md`, `drills-1.8.md` and AUTHORING section 5.
Method: as `review-drills-ci-7.md`. I read each drill as a candidate (brief, constraints, hint, examples) and wrote my own solution to all 15 before reading the model (`mine.py`). I ran mine and the model on every example and hidden test, both against a brute force of my own on 400 small random inputs a drill, and against each other on 600 larger ones (`checks.py random`). I evaluated the real arguments of all 240 calls and tested them against the prose constraints and the `constraints` objects (`checks.py constraints`). I wrote 84 wrong or too-slow candidates that are neither planted nor among the author's 83, and 21 right ones in other styles (`wrong.py`, `right.py`); ran them natively; searched small inputs for the shortest test that catches each one that passed (`verify_passers.py`, `search_tests.py`); and ran the ones that mattered on the drill page (Chromium and Pyodide) through `planted-check.mjs` on a scratch copy of `app/` (`page_probe.py`). Timings are Pyodide 0.27.7 under node, all hidden tests in one run (`pyo_time.py`).
Scripts: `docs/reviews/drills-ci-8-scripts/` (from the repo root, with the scratchpad venv Python: `harness.py mine.py|wrong.py|right.py app/content/drills [--limit 15]`, `checks.py app/content/drills random|constraints`, `verify_passers.py`, `search_tests.py app/content/drills`, `planted_reason.py app/content/drills`, `depth.py app/content/drills`, `extra_checks.py app/content/drills . [--proposed]`, `resize.py app/content/drills`, `pyo_time.py app/content/drills <pkgs> <labels>`; browser runs through `page_probe.py` on a copy; proposed tests in `extra_tests.json`).
Format: `N. file | JSON path | tag | what is wrong | exact fix`. Tags: `wrong` = a learner is marked wrongly or told something false that matters; `unclear` = the statement leaves a graded case open; `weak` = the tests miss a real wrong or too-slow solution; `minor` = otherwise.

## Tool results

- Author's script: `drills-ci-8-check.py` ends `15 drills, 0 errors` (4 min 9 s natively). I did not run its `--pyodide` mode; my own timer repeats its table (below).
- My own solutions: 15 of 15 pass every example and hidden test. **No `expect` is wrong.** Model, mine and a brute force on 6,000 small random inputs, model and mine on 9,000 larger ones: 0 disagreements. Second methods where one exists: a sliding minimum over doubled prefix sums (loop starts), a sliding minimum over `[j - hi, j - lo]` (stone hops), traders in score order (ring bonuses), ordered pairs halved (one bit apart), numbers below `n + 1` (k-bit count).
- Constraints: 240 calls read with their real arguments, 0 outside the prose or the objects (sorted bookings that do not overlap, `need` at most the job's length, `k` at most `rows * cols`, `lo <= hi <= n`, one `R`, the stdin line counts). Prose and objects agree except the four small points of finding 15.
- One answer only. Interval ends are stated in every interval drill (closed in `ci-grace-sessions` and `ci-double-checks`, half-open in `ci-first-fit-slot` and `ci-room-numbers`) and each has a test on the boundary. Ties: `ci-room-numbers` fixes equal starts by list order and tests it three times; in `ci-double-checks` the order of jobs with equal ends does not change the count (mine sorts them the other way). Bit width: 30 bits and 60 bits, stated and tested at the top bit. `ci-stop-cascade` says "one such stop fires" without saying which: the order cannot change a line of output, because prices only rise inside a cascade, and my brute force fires a random ready stop and agrees on 6,000 inputs. The open cases I found are findings 9 and 12.
- `node tools/planted-check.mjs --drill <15 ids> --require --local-cdn <pkgs>` in three runs: 15 drills checked, 65 planted solutions, 0 failures. `planted_reason.py`: every wrong plant first fails on a wrong value (two on the exception their slip describes) and every slow plant is still running after 6 s on a large test and right on the small ones. Each fails for the reason its `slip` gives.
- **My unplanted candidates: 84 wrong or too slow. 74 are caught by a hidden test** (a wrong value, an exception, or a run past the limit). **8 pass every example and every hidden test in Chromium** (findings 1 to 5), and **2 more pass every hidden test and fail only a visible example**, which Submit does not run (findings 5 and 6). With the tests of `extra_tests.json` put in, the model passes and all ten fail on the page (`page_probe.py --tests`: 6 drills, 10 candidates, 0 failures), and every planted solution and every one of the author's 83 still fails (`extra_checks.py --proposed`).
- Right solutions in other styles: 21 in `right.py`, my own 15, and 15 more that I wrote as wrong and that proved right on 6,000 random inputs each (`EQUIVALENT` in `wrong.py`). All pass natively; the ones I ran on the page pass there too (the three `bisect` forms, the sorted free list, the digit recursion, the unvisited list for stone hops), except the two recursions of findings 7 and 13.
- **Recursion depth** (`depth.py`). No test puts a natural recursion between 300 and 1,000 frames, the band that hurt pack 7. Two tests go far past it: `ci-ring-bonuses` tests[11] and [12] (50,001 frames for "one more than my lower neighbour") and `ci-stop-cascade` tests[14] (a cascade of 20,000 stops). Every other test of those two drills stays under 30 frames, and no other drill has a natural recursion deeper than its 60 bits, 14 switches or 50 rings. On the page: the `lru_cache` recursion for ring bonuses passes 11 of 15 (it stops at tests[11] and the rest are skipped); the same recursion with a dict and the limit raised passes all 15, 50,000 deep; the stop-cascade recursion fails tests[14] only (an ordinary `RecursionError` in Pyodide under node) and passes with the limit raised.
- **Timings, difficulty 4 and 5** (seconds for all hidden tests; the page's limit is 5). Models: k-bit count 0.04, loop starts 0.25, ring bonuses 0.30, room numbers 0.30, spiral cells 0.26, stone hops 0.26, stop cascade 0.41: every one at least twelve times inside the limit. Obvious methods: `accumulate` per depot, the hop table, and stops sorted after every event are still running at 25 s; `min` over a set of free rooms takes 14.7 s, the nearest of the plants at 2.9 times the limit. So the obvious method fails and the target passes with room in all seven.
- **The two `bisect` drills.** Free rooms in a sorted list with `insort` and `pop(0)`: 0.72 s (the report says 0.75). Busy and free rooms both that way: 1.39 s. Book and stops in sorted lists with `pop(0)`: 1.48 s (the report's form took 1.09); kept in falling order and popped from the end: 0.64 s. All pass on the page here. The room left is 3.4 to 7.8 times, under the brief's factor of five in two of the four forms, so a browser three and a half times slower than this machine refuses them after being told "fast enough at this size". Finding 10.
- `how` test counts match the `tests` arrays in all 15; `tags[0]` is the pack; mix 3 / 5 / 5 / 2; two stdin drills. Notes and `how` lines checked against the code: the double-checks model inserts at most 4 places from the end of its list over all tests (notes[2] holds); the robot moves at most `max(r, c)` cells a command; a switch set costs at most 14 XORs; the spiral search is 30 rounds for $5 \times 10^8$ rings; $\binom{60}{30}$ = 118,264,581,564,861,424 is tests[6]. - No drill repeats a `coding-int`, `trees` or `contests` code task (I listed every starter function of the three courses; nearest are `merge_intervals`, `run_book`, `spiral_order`, `rotate`, `min_rooms`, `single_number`, all different tasks) or a `ci-` drill of packs 1 to 7. `ci-loop-starts` is the sequel of `dsa-loop-depots` in the same words (finding 14). `ci-stone-hops` and pack 2's `ci-hop-score` can both be solved with a sliding window over earlier positions; the asks differ (fewest hops between `lo` and `hi` over water, best total over 1 to `k`).
- Classics, said plainly. Eleven drills are known tasks with a change that changes the code: merge intervals with a grace gap and a count; task scheduler II with a rest per machine and every day returned; the walking robot with edges and a count of cells; rotating the rings of a grid with alternate directions; "set intersection size at least two" with any `need` up to 5; insert interval turned into a first-fit search; meeting rooms with cleaning time and the room number of each meeting; the gas station asked for every start; jump game VII (a string, a shortest and longest jump) asked for the fewest hops; candy on a circle; spiral order asked for one step on a field of $10^{18}$ plots.
