# Concept audit: vectors (topic 1.4, Stage 1), levels 1 to 3, round 1

Scope: `app/content/courses/vectors/` l1 to l12, review-1 to review-3, homework-1 to homework-3, problems-1 to problems-3, fluency-l3, fluency-l5, fluency-l7 and fluency-l10, at commit 397dd192. Blind read: no earlier review, audit or author report on this course was opened until the ledger and the findings below were written (commit "blind part" on this branch). Ledger: `docs/reports/audit-vectors-1-3-ledger.md` (224 rows: 210 correct, 3 wrong, 9 imprecise, 2 misleading, 0 unverified). Scripts: `docs/reports/vectors-L1-3-concepts.py` (78 checks; exits 1, with 2 checks failing because the content is wrong: findings 1 and 2) and `docs/reports/vectors-L1-3-concepts-sweep.mjs` (59 codings of named mistakes run against the 7 Python widgets under the page's Pyodide 0.27.7, NumPy 2.0.2, plus 19 probes of what that NumPy prints).

Method: every definition and rule was set against Strang, Axler or Anton and against a NumPy recomputation; every "always / only / never" claim got a random or exhaustive search for a counterexample (Cauchy-Schwarz, the triangle equality case, sign and angle, span, both views of a matrix product, the transpose and inverse rules, determinant as signed area factor, the count of solutions of singular systems, the shear and turn orders on every slider setting). Empirical claims got a simulation: portfolio variance against 2,000,000 simulated returns, minimum-variance weights against 40,000 fully invested rivals, the size of daily covariance determinants, the Hilbert residuals, what NumPy raises and prints. Every `feedback.byKey.test-N` line was swept with two or more natural codings of its mistake.

Format: `N. file | JSON path | severity, tag | problem | evidence and basis | exact fix`. Fourteen findings: 3 wrong, 2 misleading, 9 imprecise; 2 serious (1 and 3), 12 minor. Numbers run in order of weight, so they are not in page order.

## Checked and clean

Level 1: span and "parallel" (exhaustive over integer pairs, the zero vector counted as a multiple, as the definition says); the algebraic dot product and its sign against the angle (100,000 pairs in 2 to 5 dimensions; the items that need it say "non-zero"); "zero dot product between non-zero arrows means perpendicular" (stated with the hypothesis each time); Cauchy-Schwarz as used in l2 screens[0] and l3 screens[8]; the cosine formula against the cosine rule and `atan2`; unit vectors; the projection $k = \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} / \mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{v}$, its direction (onto $\mathbf{v}$, a multiple of $\mathbf{v}$) and the reverse slip; correlation as the cosine of the demeaned vectors against `np.corrcoef`, and beta as the least-squares slope with intercept (5,000 samples); $R_p = \mathbf{w} \cdot \mathbf{r}$ against pound values for long, short and leveraged books, weights as shares of own money summing to 1; P&L as $\mathbf{q} \cdot \Delta\mathbf{p}$; every slip value. Convention: returns are simple returns throughout, fixed by l4 screens[8] ("return is gain over starting value"); log returns are never mentioned, and the script confirms the identity is false for them, so nothing here contradicts later courses. No norm other than the Euclidean length is named, and no cross product appears.

Level 2: both views of $\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}$; $\mathbf{A}\mathbf{e}_j$; the rotation matrix and its sense; shear, stretch, the three reflections, the flattening matrix; "the two orders give the same shape only when $k = 0$ or $\theta$ is 0° or 180°" on all 91 slider settings, comparing both the matrices and the image squares; $(\mathbf{AB})\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{A}(\mathbf{B}\mathbf{x})$; the shape rule; $(\mathbf{AB})^T = \mathbf{B}^T\mathbf{A}^T$ for non-square factors; $\mathbf{A}^T\mathbf{A}$ symmetric; $\mathbf{w}^T\boldsymbol{\Sigma}\mathbf{w}$ against simulation (the 2,000,000 draws separate 0.01888 from the slips 0.01744 and 0.016); "below the weighted average" for long-only mixes; the factor-hedge and bakery chains.

Level 3: rows as equations and columns as unknowns; the $2 \times 2$ inverse on 50,000 matrices; $(\mathbf{AB})^{-1} = \mathbf{B}^{-1}\mathbf{A}^{-1}$ and the failure of the same-order product; determinant as signed area factor for the unit square and a triangle; $\det(\mathbf{AB})$; "singular means no solution or infinitely many, never one" over every small integer case; the minimum-variance formula; the singular covariance example; every NumPy statement, on the page's NumPy and on desktop: the printed determinant, the `A * x` array, the Hilbert residuals (4.4e-16 and 5.2e-05 reproduce exactly under Pyodide; desktop NumPy 2.5 prints 9.7e-05 for the second), `nan` in the browser against `LinAlgError` on desktop, the ranks, the scaled determinants $2 \times 10^{-16}$ and $8 \times 10^{-16}$, the `np.allclose` tolerance; the bond-curve expected values.
