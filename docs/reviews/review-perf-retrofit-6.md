## The orchestrator's points

- opt-dash L4 weekend theta: three calendar days, $dt = 3/365$, with `bs_greeks` theta per year (test 4, the weekend tests 7 and 9, and the explain). The prompt states it ("over `days` calendar days", "years of 365 days"), and the course teaches the convention: l13 screen 3 converts theta to "£ per calendar day" as $-6.414/365$, and `fd_greeks` bumps $T$ by $1/365$. No lesson mentions weekends or trading-day theta, so nothing contradicts it. A weekend counted as one day fails tests 4, 7 and 9; the author's 252-day slip fails test 4.
- alpha L4 "dollar-neutral" with missing signals and ties: the book is unique under the prose. The median is over the shares with a signal; a share exactly at the median holds £0; if either side sums to 0 the date is cash. No share on any date of `five_days()`, `panel(7)` or `panel(11)` sits within $10^{-9}$ of its median without being on it, so no float tie can split two correct codings. The only other reading of "median", the lower median of an even count, is not the median and fails five tests. One gap in the stated interface (AL4-1).
- ensembles L2 hypotheses: the derivation uses one variance and one pairwise covariance; the bias step uses one mean. "Identically distributed" gives all three but is more than is used, and the required row 1 demands it (ENS-1). The limit $\rho\sigma^2$ as $B$ grows is right, with every finite forest above it while $\rho < 1$ and $\sigma^2 > 0$; the bias is unchanged by linearity, and the MSE split is derived with its cross term. The prompt's source of randomness is imprecise (ENS-2).
- contests L3 exchange: the model swaps positions $k$ and $m$ (first difference), not an adjacent inversion. Its bound is right: every job in positions $k$ to $m$ of the swapped order ends by $T$, when $g$ ended before, and is one of $g_k, \dots, g_n$, so is due no earlier than $g$; jobs outside the block keep their finish times. So each moved job, $g$ and $o$ included, is late by at most $\ell_g(O) \le L(O)$, and the maximum cannot rise. The textbook adjacent-inversion route is also valid (checked), but the required rows 2 and 4 do not accept it (CON-1).
