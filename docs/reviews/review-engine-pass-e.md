## 2. Findings

### Errors

1. **error** | `app/js/engine/player.js` 643-655 and 683 (`titleStep`, reached only for `spec.type === 'title'`) | The Refresh chip is drawn inside the `title` screen only. Of 987 written lessons, 88 start with a `title` screen; every interview lesson (research-int, coding-int, systems and the like) starts with a `problem` screen. Scenario: a learner tests out of `research-int/L2`, comes back after 40 days, opens `research-int/l11-framing-the-question`: `ZQ.prereq.refreshFor` returns `[{course:'research-int', n:2, review:'review-2', days:40}]` (checked), but the page shows the first problem and no chip. The brief (section 6) says "on the dependent lesson's start screen". About 91 percent of lessons never show it. Fix: render the chip from the player's mount, above the first step whatever its type (a small strip in the lesson header), and keep `titleStep` free of it; add a `--routes-only` check that ages a met record 31 days and finds `.rf-chip` on a lesson with a non-title first screen.

2. **error** | `app/js/prereq.js` 113-126 (`levelLocked`, `lessonLocked`, `itemLocked`) | A level that is met stays locked as a place to work if its own references are unmet. Scenario (reproduced): fresh profile, `testout` of `research-int/L2` recorded as passed. `levelMet` is true and the L3 lesson opens, but all five L2 lessons, `review-2` and `homework-2` report locked, and `#review/research-int/review-2` shows "Level 2 review is locked". The Refresh chip would link to exactly that locked review. The learner has "met" a level whose content they cannot open, cannot redo, and from which the card cannot say anything more (nothing is missing). The brief says nothing unlocked ever locks and a met level stays met; a met level should be open. Fix: in `levelLocked` return false when `levelMet(cid, n)` is true (the met record is sticky), and in `lessonLocked` and `itemLocked` apply the same early return for lessons without a `lessonRequires` entry. Add a routes check: after a test-out of a level with unmet references, its review opens.

### Warnings

3. **warning** | `app/js/prereq.js` 134-138 (`examLocked`), `app/js/screens/exam.js` 81-85, `app/content/exams/mock-*.json` | A mock locks only when its file carries a top-level `requires`; none of the 18 mocks does (`grep -l '"requires"' app/content/exams/*.json` finds nothing). So on a fresh profile every mock is open, and Prepare never shows a mock as Locked. The brief (section 5) says a mock whose papers draw from locked levels shows the locked card naming them. The engine decision (field on the mock) is documented, but the content to make it work is missing and nothing asks for it. Fix: either derive the set from the mock's items (lessons named by `requires` or by `lesson` refs in `parts`/`draw`), or have the mapping pass write `requires` on each mock and add a validator warning for a `mock-*` file that draws or lists items from courses but has no `requires`. Record the choice in DECISIONS.

4. **warning** | `app/js/prereq.js` 114-120 and 233-236 | Grandfathering covers only levels whose written lessons are all finished. A learner (old save, or any save when a later content pass adds `requires`) who finished 2 of 5 lessons of a level whose references are unmet keeps those two but finds lessons 3 to 5 locked (reproduced: `research-int` level 3, one lesson finished, `lessonLocked` is `[false, true, true, true, true]`). The brief's aim is that finished work never locks; a started level is work in progress. Fix: in `sync`, when grandfathering (and later whenever a lesson is finished), record a level with at least one finished lesson as met-by-start (`how: 'started'`) so that its remaining lessons, review and homework stay open; it need not count as met for dependants, so use a separate list or `opened`-style flag that You does not count.

5. **warning** | `app/js/prereq.js` 182-192 (`walk` in `missingRefs`) | Direct and indirect depend on list order. Walking direct reference A recurses into its own references before the next direct reference B is looked at; if A needs B, B lands in `indirect` and the later pass over `rs` skips it because it is in `seen`. The card then shows B only under "and N more", not under "Finish these first", although the level lists it. Not triggered by the present mapping (checked: no level has a direct reference reachable through another direct one), but the next mapping edit or a `requires: [A, B]` written in that order triggers it. Fix: first pass puts every unmet reference of `refs` into `direct` and `seen`; second pass walks their references with depth 1.

6. **warning** | `app/js/screens/review.js` 695-696 | The test-out seed is `hash(key + '/' + tries)` and the draw is 6 of the review's 6 to 10 items. For a 6-item review the "shuffled" retake is the same 6 items; for 10 items it overlaps by at least 2 and, on average, 3.6. After a miss the summary lists every problem (stem and right or wrong), so the learner can look the answers up and retake after 24 hours with mostly the same items. Fix: store the ids already shown in the test-out record (`seen`), draw unseen items first, and, when the review has fewer than 12 items, say in AUTHORING that a test-out needs a review of at least 8 items to give a different second paper (or relax the validator's review range to a minimum of 8 for levels that other levels require).

7. **warning** | `app/js/prereq.js` 76-93 (`derivedMet`) | A level with no written review is met when every written lesson is finished, even when some lessons are not yet written. Dependants open before the level is complete, and the validator's warning ("no written lesson yet: stays locked until written") fires only when no lesson at all is written. Today the data keeps this latent (no written dependent points at an unwritten level, checked), but a half-written level will quietly satisfy its dependants. Fix: require every lesson on the map to be written for this branch, or add a validator warning for a referenced level that is partly written and has no review.

### Notes

8. **note** | `app/js/prereq.js` 113-132 | Performance is fine. Measured in Chromium with the real mapping on a fresh profile: `lessonLocked` for all 1,214 lessons 15 ms; one deep `missingFor` (84 nodes) 1.8 ms; `sync` 0.8 ms (it runs on every state event). The full `missingFor` over every locked lesson takes 0.6 s, but nothing does that on a render. No caching needed now; if Learn ever draws a lock per course card, memoise per state version.

9. **note** | `app/tools/validate.mjs` 1906 and 1936-1937 | (a) A level `requires` naming a lesson of its own level is allowed (`!lessonRef` skips the self check); the level then waits for its own lesson, which is locked by the level, a deadlock the validator does not report (none in the real data). Fix: error when a level's `requires` names its own level or one of its lessons. (b) `lessonRequires` adds an edge from the lesson's whole level, so a lesson that needs less than its level can show as a cycle although the engine would not deadlock. Fix: draw the edge from the lesson node, or say so in AUTHORING.

10. **note** | `app/js/screens/locked.js` 21-24 | For a missing level with no written lesson and no written review the row reads "0 of 0 lessons finished, 4 not yet written, no review written yet" with the hint "Finish its lessons to unlock it". The hint contradicts the row. Latent (30 validator warnings, but no written level depends on an unwritten one). Fix: when `lessonsWritten === 0`, say "Not yet written: this opens when it is written" and hide Test out.

11. **note** | `DECISIONS.md` line 75 and `docs/briefs/prereq-gating.md` section 1 | The decisions that differ from the brief live only in the engine report: top-level `lessonRequires` for the brief's "lesson map entry", an optional `requires` on mocks, review pass default 0.7 and `pass` on a review, the met cut at the first met level, a level without a review met by its written lessons. Fix: one DECISIONS bullet listing them, and a one-line correction in the brief's section 1.

12. **note** | `app/js/screens/today.js` 212 | The "Jump back in" level strip picks the next lesson in the level without the lock test, so "Start level n" can open a locked card (the card is correct, the label promises a lesson). Fix: reuse `nextLesson(Z, cid)` here and show "See what is missing" when it is null.

13. **note** | `app/tools/playthrough.mjs` (`gatingChecks`, around 1245-1262) | The card check compares the rows with `ZQ.prereq.missingFor`, the code under test, so a wrong closure would pass. Fix: add the independent check (read `course.json`, compute the closure in Node, compare row refs); I did this by hand for `research-int/l11` and it matched, so the check would pass today.

14. **note** | `app/js/prereq.js` 82-83 | A passed stage exit test meets every level of its stage, whatever the test covers (only `stage-0` exists). The brief says "covering that topic". Fine for one test per stage; if a stage test is split by topic, add a `covers` list and read it here.

15. **note** | `app/js/prereq.js` 100-103 (`testOutFacts`) | The 24-hour wait is `at + 24 h` in epoch milliseconds, so time zones and daylight saving cannot change it. A device clock set back extends the wait; there is no trusted clock to cap it, so leave it.

This is a mapping matter (brief section 8: interview courses gate only on what their briefs name) rather than an engine defect; the content second reader should check that research-int level 1 and 2 need the whole core chain. Layout at 1280 and 390 px is clean (no horizontal overflow, 44 px targets, indirect groups collapsed).
